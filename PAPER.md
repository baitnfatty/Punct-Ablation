# Punctuation Ablation Under Attention-Matched Controls: A Pre-registered Null, a Positive Control, and a Key-Pathway Localization

## Abstract

Punctuation & Predicates (P&P, arXiv 2508.14067) reported that punctuation tokens are causally necessary for RuleTaker-style classification in small models fine-tuned on the task, using representation-level zeroing with no control conditions. We ask two questions. First, does a punctuation-specific effect appear in a non-fine-tuned instruct model reasoning zero-shot with chain of thought? Second, does P&P's effect survive a control that matches attention received rather than token class? Under a pre-registered protocol with a mechanical kill criterion, knocking out all attention into prompt punctuation in Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 produced no punctuation-specific excess damage detectable at the design's sensitivity (paired contrast versus attention-matched non-punctuation positions: −0.58 logits, 95% CI [−1.20, +0.04], n=167; minimum detectable effect ≈1.4 logits). The paired means reproduce to ~0.03 on an independent hardware and software stack (A100, fp32). The same harness detects a large effect where the literature says one exists: in GPT-2-124M fine-tuned on the task family, write-ablating punctuation positions collapses analysis-set accuracy from 1.000 to 0.273. However, about 97% of that gross effect is reproduced by attention-matched non-punctuation controls (implied control accuracy 0.279; margin damage −9.83 versus −10.10), leaving only a small punctuation-specific residual on the margin metric (−0.27, [−0.55, +0.01]). A pre-registered key/value decomposition localizes the fine-tuned model's collapse to the key projection: substituting the write-ablated input into keys at punctuation positions drops accuracy to chance (0.364) while the same substitution into values preserves it (0.988). We conclude that the uncontrolled "punctuation is necessary" claim is largely a claim about heavily-attended positions in general, that no punctuation-specific routing effect is detectable zero-shot at this sensitivity, and that where punctuation does matter in the fine-tuned regime its causal weight is in addressing, not content.

## 1. Background and question

P&P zero the full activation of punctuation tokens, layer by layer, in models fine-tuned on RuleTaker-style classification (GPT-2-small full fine-tune; DeepSeek-1.3B and Gemma-2B via LoRA) and find punctuation necessary and sufficient. Zeroing a position removes its identity, its position, and its formed content together, and the paper compares punctuation only against the intact model. Two prior lines make that comparison ambiguous. The attention-sink literature (arXiv 2402.17762, 2402.09221) shows that delimiter positions attract structural attention with near-zero, largely input-invariant value content, so damage from removing them may reflect their role as addressing targets rather than any punctuation-specific content. LLM-Microscope (arXiv 2502.15007) makes the opposite, correlational claim: punctuation hidden states are highly contextualized and act as context memory. Neither line has been tested with a causal intervention that holds attention received constant.

We designed the smallest experiment that separates these accounts: intervene on punctuation positions and on non-punctuation positions matched for attention received, in both a zero-shot instruct model and a fine-tuned small model, and then, where an effect exists, split it into key (addressing) and value (content) pathways.

## 2. Method

**Stimuli.** 180 RuleTaker-style theories with computed labels (seed 0), generated by `studies/make_ruletaker_punct.py`. Analysis sets condition on baseline-correct items (n=167 for the zero-shot model, n=165 for the fine-tuned model).

**Interventions** (`gentrace/intervene.py`). Knockout (KO) zeros attention into a set of key positions at all layers. Write-ablation (WA) replaces a position's formed residual content with its embedding-only state at all layers, preserving embedding and rotary position. Both are verified mechanically exact on both architectures (write-zeroing residual delta 0.0; knockout attention-into-key 0.0). The tool is an ungated forward-mutation instrument; no GenTrace gate certifies any conclusion drawn with it.

**Controls.** For each item, non-punctuation positions are selected to match the attention received by the punctuation positions (`matched_control` in `studies/run_punct_ablation.py`). The matched set is content-bearing (it includes subject and query-attribute tokens), so the contrast bounds punctuation-specialness against equally-attended content positions rather than isolating attention profile per se.

**Metrics.** Correct-label logit at the readout position (primary) and the correct-versus-best-other margin (co-primary, shift-invariant). Paired bootstrap CIs, sign tests and Wilcoxon tests are computed by `studies/analyze_gate_nonparam.py`.

**Pre-registration chain, stated exactly.** The zero-shot protocol (`prereg/PREREG_punct_mechanism.md` v2.1) is git-anchored frozen before its result (commit d45306f at 09:40 on 2026-07-08, result at 09:45); the kill criterion was mechanical. For the GPT-2 control (`prereg/PREREG_gpt2_control.md`), the interpretation cells are git-anchored five minutes before the ablation artifacts; that training was declared before it ran, and that amendment A1 preceded any WA number, are self-attested and structurally corroborated (the anchored runner was KO-only, so no WA code path existed). The key/value direction was git-anchored 74 minutes before its result and before the `ablate_kv` code existed.

**Determinism.** Byte-identical pairs were obtained for the pre-registered 5-item subset of the zero-shot gate on the local fp16 stack, for the full-N fp32 reproduction on an A100, and for the full GPT-2 control run after a deterministic-algorithms fix. Every artifact records `shortcuts_taken: []`.

**Models and hardware.** Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (zero-shot, CoT), captured on an AMD RX 6900 XT in fp16 under ROCm and reproduced on an NVIDIA A100 in fp32. GPT-2-124M fine-tuned for one epoch on 8001 generated training items (validation accuracy 0.329 → 0.976; proceed gate ≥ 0.90), disjoint from the 180 study items.

## 3. Results

### 3.1 C-A: no detectable punctuation-specific effect zero-shot

Knocking out all attention into prompt punctuation, versus attention-matched non-punctuation positions, gives a paired logit contrast of −0.58 (95% CI [−1.20, +0.04], n=167; sign p=.02, Wilcoxon p=.08). The co-primary margin is flat (+0.22, [−0.34, +0.80], sign p=.88). Raw KO damage versus baseline is −2.39 for punctuation and −1.82 for matched controls. The pre-registered gate returned KILL. This is a failure to detect at the stated sensitivity (MDE ≈ 1.4 logits), not an equivalence finding; the logit contrast leans negative.

The paired means reproduce on an independent stack (A100, cuda/float32, same harness, same frozen fp16-generated CoT baselines): −0.61 [−1.23, +0.02]. Per-item contrasts differ by up to ~2.9 logits between fp16 and fp32.

### 3.2 C-B: the harness detects a large effect where one exists

In the fine-tuned GPT-2, write-ablating punctuation positions at all layers moves the correct-label logit by −22.69 on average and collapses analysis-set accuracy from 1.000 to 0.273, below three-way chance (baseline accuracy over all 180 items is 0.917). The KO arm is also sensitive: knocking out attention-matched controls costs −11.2 logits and drops accuracy to 0.739, so the zero-shot null in 3.1 is not KO-arm blindness.

### 3.3 C-C: what survives the control P&P lacked

Write-ablating the attention-matched non-punctuation controls reproduces about 97% of the gross effect: implied accuracy 0.279 versus 0.273 for punctuation, margin damage −9.83 versus −10.10. A small punctuation-specific residual on the margin metric is indicated but not established (−0.27, [−0.55, +0.01]; punctuation more damaging in 102 of 165 items, sign p=.003, Wilcoxon p≈.045). The +4.76 logit-scale contrast reported at the logit level is shift-sensitive (write-ablation moves the whole logit vector; SD 16.6 versus 1.9 for the margin) and carries no directional weight.

### 3.4 C-D: knockout versus write-ablation (descriptive)

Blocking all reads of punctuation positions in the fine-tuned model costs little (accuracy 1.000 → 0.964, margin −1.14), while write-ablating them collapses the model. This asymmetry motivated the decomposition in 3.5; on its own it is descriptive.

### 3.5 C-E: the collapse flows through the key pathway

Substituting the write-ablated (embedding-only) residual into each attention projection's input at punctuation positions, one projection at a time, in the fine-tuned model:

| Cell | Accuracy | Margin |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 1.000 | — |
| Full write-ablation | 0.273 | −10.10 |
| Keys ← WA, values intact | 0.364 | −9.70 |
| Values ← WA, keys intact | 0.988 | −5.21 |
| Keys ← 0 | 0.527 | — |
| Values ← 0 | 0.915 | — |

Key substitution recovers most of the full-WA collapse: accuracy falls to chance and majority-class rate (0.364), with a ~9% residual gap above full-WA's below-chance floor. Value substitution preserves the answer, though its margin cost (−5.21) means values are near-inert rather than provably zero. Accuracy and margin agree in direction (keys more damaging than values in 156 of 165 items). A specifically wrong key (0.364) hurts more than an absent one (0.527). The damage therefore flows through the key/addressing pathway; no attention-pattern readout was taken, so this is a pathway localization, not a claim about re-routing to specific positions.

### 3.6 Exploratory: during-generation knockout (n=21)

To address the concern that the zero-shot readout can copy an answer already stated in the frozen CoT, the pre-registered exploratory complement ran knockout active throughout generation. Punctuation knockout flipped the generated answer 4.8% of the time (CoT edit distance 0.48); attention-matched control knockout flipped it 71.4% of the time (edit distance 0.84). This is consistent with 3.1 and 3.3 but carries no confirmatory weight and has not had its own adversarial review.

## 4. What this does and does not establish

**Does.** In one zero-shot instruct model, no punctuation-specific read-side effect is detectable at MDE ≈ 1.4 logits, and this reproduces across hardware and precision. In one fine-tuned GPT-2, the gross necessity effect is real and large, is ~97% reproduced by equally-attended content positions, and flows predominantly through the key projection.

**Does not.** Generalize beyond these two checkpoints, one task family and one stimulus generator. Address P&P's layer-resolved claims (all interventions are whole-class, all-layers). Prove values are exactly inert. Establish attention re-routing. Establish equivalence in the zero-shot case. Certify any mechanistic interpretation: a verified intervention certifies the arithmetic of a contrast, never its reading.

**Limits carried from the note.** Newlines were excluded from the punctuation class by a tokenizer-strip artifact. Write-ablation is the closest analog to P&P's zeroing in this harness, not a faithful replication (P&P zero full single-layer activations). The pre-registration is a trust mechanism, not a validity mechanism: the zero-shot protocol is git-anchored; the GPT-2 chain is substantially self-attested.

## 5. Relation to prior work

Against P&P: the gross necessity effect replicates in regime, and the missing control accounts for most of it; P&P pose no zero-shot transfer question, and our null speaks only to the read-side (knockout) form in one zero-shot model. Against the attention-sink line: C-E gives a causal localization of the parked-attention account in one fine-tuned model, where the delimiter's causal weight sits in its key and its value is near-inert. Against LLM-Microscope: the correlational "punctuation as context memory" claim finds no support at the frozen-CoT readout in the zero-shot regime and only a small margin residual in the fine-tuned regime; causal tests at other readouts remain open.
